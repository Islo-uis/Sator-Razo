Our Heritage

Boutique Excellence
Rooted in Community

Established in 2007 in Tagum City, Davao del Norte, Sator & Razo Law Firm was founded on the principle that exceptional legal representation should be accessible, transparent, and unyielding.

We intentionally maintain a boutique practice structure. This ensures that every client receives our dedicated focus, allowing us to blend sharp litigation strategies with a genuine commitment to the welfare of our local community.
